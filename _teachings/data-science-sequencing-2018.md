I co-developed and helped teach this course with my PhD advisor [David Tse](https://web.stanford.edu/~dntse/) and Jesse M. Zhang. The course covers various problems in computational genomics, the different ways of modelling them and solving them, with tools from statistics, computer science and information theory that have been instrumental in tackling these problems.
